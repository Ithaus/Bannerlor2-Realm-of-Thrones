# PLAYER DESCRIPTION GUIDE
# ROT AI Influence Community Pack
# ================================

## WHAT IS playerdescription.txt?

The playerdescription.txt file lets you tell the AI who your character is.
Everything you write here will be known to ALL NPCs in every conversation.

Think of it as your character's permanent identity card — not your current
campaign status (the mod handles that automatically), but who your character
IS as a person: their name, house, background, personality, and how the world
should react to them.

The file is hot-reloaded automatically. No restart needed after saving.


## WHAT GOES HERE vs. WHAT GOES ELSEWHERE

playerdescription.txt  →  Your character's identity, background, personality,
                           house history, and how NPCs should react to them.

world_info.json        →  Public facts about your house that ALL NPCs might
                           know as world knowledge (optional, for advanced users).

world_secrets.json     →  Hidden secrets connected to your character or house
                           that only certain NPCs might discover (optional).

world.txt              →  Do NOT add character info here. This file is for
                           world lore only. Editing it affects all NPCs globally.


## HOW TO USE

1. Create a new file called exactly:  playerdescription.txt
2. Place it in your AIInfluence mod folder (same folder as world.txt)
3. Fill it in using the template below — in English
4. Save the file. It will be loaded automatically on the next dialogue.

You do not need to fill in every section.
Leave a section blank or delete it entirely if you prefer the AI to discover
that aspect of your character through conversation instead.


## TIPS FOR GOOD RESULTS

- Be specific rather than vague. "A calculating lord who prefers diplomacy
  but is capable of ruthlessness when necessary" works better than "smart."

- Write the NPC reaction section carefully. This is where you shape how the
  world treats your character — it has a strong effect on immersion.

- Keep it honest to how you actually play. If you change your playstyle
  mid-campaign, update the file.

- You can update the file at any time. Changes take effect on the next dialogue.

- Do not write things the AI already knows from game data — your current
  kingdom, your fiefs, your troops. Focus on personality and backstory.

--- HOUSE AND ORIGIN ---

[Describe your house or family and where it comes from. How old is the house?
What is it known for? Is it powerful, minor, ancient, newly risen?
Where is your ancestral seat or home — a castle in Westeros, a villa in one
of the Free Cities of Essos, a khalasar on the Dothraki Sea, or elsewhere?
What is your house or family's reputation among lords, merchants, or smallfolk
in your region of the world?]


--- PERSONAL HISTORY ---

[Describe your character's background. Where did they grow up?
What significant events shaped them? Have they experienced loss, war,
exile, or great fortune? What drives them forward?
Keep it grounded — specific details work better than grand summaries.]


--- NOTABLE POSSESSIONS ---

[List anything notable your character carries or owns that NPCs might
recognize or react to — a Valyrian steel sword, a famous horse, a
signet ring, a maester's chain, etc. Include the name and a brief
description of its significance.]


--- CURRENT STATUS ---

[Describe your character's current situation in the game world.
Do they serve a great house? Are they a free agent? A wandering knight?
A lord without lands? An exile seeking restoration?
This gives NPCs context for how to place your character politically.]


--- PERSONALITY ---

[Describe how your character thinks and behaves. Are they honorable,
calculating, impulsive, patient, cruel, generous, ambitious?
What do they want? What do they fear? What are they willing to do
and what are they not willing to do?
This shapes how NPCs read your character in conversation.]


--- HOW NPCS SHOULD REACT ---

[This is one of the most important sections. Describe specifically how
different types of NPCs should respond to your character.

Examples of what to cover:
- Do lords of a certain region know your house? How do they feel about it?
- Does your character have a reputation — good or bad — that precedes them?
- Are there people who owe your character something, or who have a grudge?
- How should strangers treat your character on first meeting?
- Is there anything about your character that NPCs would find unusual,
  impressive, suspicious, or unsettling?]

## EXAMPLE — EDWARD RAVEN (for reference) This is my own but still AI generated.
================================================================================

PLAYER CHARACTER INFO:

Name: Edward Raven
Age: 20
House: House Raven of Brandybottom, The Reach
Sigil: A black raven with a red eye on a white field
House Words: We Remember Beneath Fair Skies

{Player} = Edward Raven


--- HOUSE AND ORIGIN ---

House Raven was a noble house of The Reach, with their ancestral seat at
Brandybottom — a modest but beloved holdfast in the fertile lands of the
Reach, not far from Highgarden. As bannermen of House Tyrell, they were
widely regarded as one of the most honorable and good-hearted houses in
the Reach. The lords of Raven were known for their generosity to the
smallfolk, their fairness in judgment, and their long memories — both for
debts owed and favors given. Their name was known as far as King's Landing
and spoken of in some of the Free Cities through trade and diplomacy.


--- PERSONAL HISTORY ---

Eight years ago, when Edward was only eight years old, Brandybottom burned
to the ground in a devastating fire. His entire family perished — his father
Harwin, his mother Eleonora, his brothers Tomard and Domeric, and his sister
Edwyna. Edward alone survived. He has been alone since childhood, carrying
his family's memory and the responsibility of being the last of his line.


--- NOTABLE POSSESSIONS ---

Beak — the ancestral Valyrian steel sword of House Raven, named after a
raven's beak. The blade is dark as midnight, razor-sharp, and never loses
its edge. It has been passed down through the Raven family for generations.
Edward carries it always. Many powerful lords covet such a blade.


--- CURRENT STATUS ---

Edward Raven serves no kingdom and has sworn fealty to no great house.
He is a free agent in the politics of Westeros — which some will find
useful and others will find suspicious.


--- PERSONALITY ---

Edward carries his family's values — honesty, fairness, long memory — but
the world has also taught him that goodness alone does not guarantee survival.
He is not naive, but he is not yet cynical. He does not talk about his family
easily, but when he does, it matters. He is young and still finding his place,
but those who underestimate him do so at their own risk.


--- HOW NPCS SHOULD REACT ---

- NPCs from the Reach or who knew House Raven will speak warmly of the family's
  legendary reputation. Many will mourn the tragedy of Brandybottom. Some who
  knew his father Harwin personally may be visibly moved to meet his son.

- NPCs who notice the sword Beak will recognize it as Valyrian steel. Reactions
  range from admiration and respect to barely concealed covetousness.

- Lords and rulers may see Edward as an opportunity — a loyal sword arm carrying
  a Valyrian steel blade, without powerful allies to protect him. Some will try
  to recruit him, some to use him, some to take what he carries.

- Merchants and travelers from Essos may have heard the Raven name through trade
  and may react with recognition.

- NPCs should treat Edward's youth with nuance — he is 20, not a child, but
  clearly still finding his place. His genuine decency and quiet grief underneath
  it should be perceptible to those who pay attention.
